About us

Creating spaces that feel deeply personal

Our story

More than interiors — a lifestyle approach

Founded with a passion for transforming ordinary spaces into meaningful experiences, My Interior has grown into a boutique design studio known for elegant planning, premium materials, and intricate detailing.

Our work celebrates texture, proportion, and purpose. We believe that beautiful spaces should feel effortless to live in and aligned with the people who inhabit them.
